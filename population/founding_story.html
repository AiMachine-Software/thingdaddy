04Novartis wrote the vision — on the wrong foundation

A tier-one pharma articulated the goal ("data from anywhere to anyone… a modular digital lab"), named the pillars (TRD Genius, Digital Lab, Digital Plant), and funded the build (Allotrope, Nerve Live, Data42). At the center of their architecture sits a data lake — while their own key-capabilities column asks for "context information (metadata)," "common terminology," and "vendor-neutral" integration.

They have the right pillars on the wrong foundation. ThingDaddy is the identity registrar their own architecture presupposes and doesn't have.

05The pallet cascade — where he wrote the rule

Retailers now mandate the pallet platform: Sam's Club's 2025 block mandate, Costco's block requirements. In EDI/SAP, "what platform does this ship-to require" is a trading-partner rule that triggers repalletization — and it's a network-effect ratchet: a supplier serving many mandating retailers can't dual-pool, so wood collapses. Each smart pallet is a GRAI; CHEP alone pools ~300 million, a moving ThingSite each.

The founder built this. The trading-partner platform rule went into SAP for PepsiCo and into RedPrairie WMS. He holds the patents, built the iGPS TAG serialization solution, invented and built the audit/repair depot for millions of failed-tag pallets, and ran the $700M CHEP-Costco pilot. And CHEP is now hiring an "IoT Serialization Specialist" — the market advertising for the thing he already invented.

06Why the discipline is the moat

Anyone can scrape. The product is the discipline that turns a scrape into a claim you can rely on: never fabricate a prefix, GLN, or GTIN; absence is a candidate, never a guess; only the answerable party ratifies. Stricter than the standard, never looser — SHACL is its formal name. That discipline is what a data lake can't provide and what makes the registry something you can build law, commerce, and medicine on top of.

The honest ledger (say it — it reads as credibility). (1) The defensible new IP must be distinguished from the founder's own prior art — the granted ThingsNet configurator especially — before public reliance; that's a job for counsel, and it's underway. (2) Trust needs the answerable parties: candidates ratify only when the owning company participates — a go-to-market motion, not a code problem. (3) Discipline example: the founding "Identity" slide shows Thermo on prefix 0614141, which is GS1's textbook example prefix — the real registry roots to a registry-verified prefix, never the placeholder. That refusal to seed truth on a convenient number is the product working.
