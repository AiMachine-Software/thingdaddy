The running order

Eight minutes, and every claim on screen is checkable while you say it.

  1. The front door. Point at the LIVE banner before anything else — the connection string, the database name, and the grade split, on screen before a single claim. "It tells you which database it is reading and how many of each grade it holds, before it shows you anything."
  2. Search a company. Two Diazyme rows come back, one rooted and one not. "Duplicates are shown, never merged silently. The registrar does not decide which company a name means — that is why the prefix is the key."
  3. Open the ThingSite. The three-authority panel is the whole argument: GS1 delegated the number space and knows nothing about the devices; a regulator attested the filing and cannot allocate a digit; and the third card is the customer. "Neither authority can produce the other's half. And neither can answer what only you know."
  4. Rebuild it in front of them. Fourteen values land one at a time, each with its source. "Airo writes you a website in minutes. We did not write yours — we found it. A generated page is plausible; a found page is checkable."
  5. The exception register. 778 named refusals — gibberish name, placeholder/test, all-zero prefix — each one a thing we looked at and could not resolve. "Every competitor hides these. Publishing them is why the other grades mean anything."
  6. The composition matrix. Four protocols against six instruments: four verified, four refused, twelve candidate, four uncheckable. Click a REFUSED cell. "The checker separated two Diazyme product lines from two declarations, without being told they existed."
  7. The MHS reference file. Layers 1 and 2 fill from the SiLA Feature. Layer 3 — enforced safety limits — shows the throughput limit marked not machine-checkable, because it is prose in a Description string. Layer 4 is who is acting, and it is empty. "MHS enforces a limit somebody typed at the rig. We can supply one the manufacturer declared, page-cited, that travels when the machine is sold."
  8. Flip to MACHINE. Same fetch, same bytes. "The page you were reading is a view of this. An agent that has never seen it gets exactly this — including every declared gap."

What to say when it shows a gap

Do not apologise for a slot. A declared gap is the product.

"Pillar 3 is empty because no method document has been read for this company. It says so, with the reason. The alternative — a page that looks complete — is the thing we exist to replace."

Known, and say it before they find it

The demo root is wrong and we know why. Verified by GS1 shows Diazyme holds 081708902 and 081730202. The register still says 0817089 — a plausible GLN parse written on 20 July labelled "GEPIR-verified", nineteen months after GEPIR was retired.

The correction is blocked, correctly. 976 of the spine's 1,127 EPC URNs cannot accept a write: they carry sgtin on class-level GTINs and fail node_urn_epc_valid, a constraint that was never validated against them. The scheme is fixed at the producer first, then the spine re-mints, then the root moves.

Say it out loud. It was found by a tool that refused to trust its own control — which is the argument, not an embarrassment.
